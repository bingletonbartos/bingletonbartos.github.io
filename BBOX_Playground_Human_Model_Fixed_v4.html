WELCOME TO BBOX PLAYGROUND

  1. Move with WASD or the left stick. Drag the screen or use the right stick to look.
  2. Get hurt on purpose: press R to ragdoll, or jump off the rooftop.
  3. Injuries and lost limbs earn cash. Spend it in the shop (B), and heal with H.
  4. Press T anytime for Quick Travel to the city, playground or rooftop.
